MobileQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-27

MobileQuest is a privacy-first, local-first app. Your journal entries, moods, sparks, and achievements live on your device by default. We do not operate servers that receive your content, we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

What the app stores (on your device)

  • Manual entries — journal lines, mood check-ins, and spark notes you type. Stored locally with SwiftData.
  • Badge progress — tiers earned, sets completed, and custom badges you create. Stored locally.

Health data (optional)

If you enable Health in Settings, MobileQuest reads only the following from Apple Health, for the last 7 days, to award movement and wellness badges:

  • Step count
  • Walking and running distance
  • Active energy burned
  • Exercise minutes (Apple Exercise Time)
  • Flights climbed
  • Mindful minutes
  • Sleep analysis (asleep duration)
  • Workout sessions (count)

This data is processed on your device only. MobileQuest never writes to Health, never uploads health data to us, and never uses health data for advertising or marketing. You can revoke Health access at any time in iOS Settings.

Screen Time (optional)

If you enable Screen Time in Settings, a DeviceActivityReport extension running on your device computes a single weekly reduction percentage. Only that percentage is shared with the main app through a private App Group. Raw app-usage data stays within Apple’s on-device Screen Time framework and is never sent to us. You can revoke authorization in iOS Settings at any time.

Journaling Suggestions (optional)

On supported devices (iOS 17.2+), you may open Apple’s Journaling Suggestions picker from the Today screen. Nothing is saved unless you actively choose a suggestion. MobileQuest does not automatically import your journal or photos.

Notifications (optional)

If you enable reminders or milestone alerts in Settings, notifications are scheduled locally on your device only. We do not use remote push notifications and notification content never leaves your device through us.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on iCloud Sync, your entries sync through your own private iCloud account (Apple CloudKit private database), tied to your Apple ID. We have no access to this data. You can turn sync off at any time. Apple’s handling of iCloud is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • No collection of your content on any server we control
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No account, sign-up, or email required to use the app

Data deletion

Because data lives on your device (and, if enabled, in your private iCloud), deleting the app removes its on-device data. To remove iCloud-synced data, turn off iCloud Sync in the app and manage the app’s iCloud storage in iOS Settings. Revoking Health or Screen Time permissions stops new reads immediately.

Children’s privacy

MobileQuest is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children and do not transmit user content off-device to us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the app’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will reflect the latest revision. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

MobileQuest 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 6 月 27 日

MobileQuest 是一款隐私优先、本地优先的应用。你的日记、心情、灵感与成就默认保存在你的设备上。我们不运营接收你内容的服务器，不使用分析或广告 SDK，也不会跨应用或网站追踪你。

应用在本机存储什么

  • 手动记录 —— 你输入的日记、心情与灵感（SwiftData，设备本地）。
  • 徽章进度 —— 已获得的等级、套装与自定徽章（设备本地）。

健康数据（可选）

若在设置中开启健康，MobileQuest 为颁发运动与健康类徽章，仅只读最近 7 天的以下数据：

  • 步数
  • 步行和跑步距离
  • 活动能量
  • 运动分钟数（Apple 运动时间）
  • 爬楼层数
  • 正念分钟
  • 睡眠分析（入睡时长）
  • 健身记录（次数）

数据仅在本机处理。MobileQuest 不会写入健康 App，不会将健康数据上传给我们，也不会将健康数据用于广告或营销。你可随时在 iOS 设置中撤销健康权限。

屏幕使用时间（可选）

若在设置中开启屏幕使用时间，设备端的 DeviceActivityReport 扩展会计算每周降低百分比。仅该百分比通过私有 App Group 与主应用共享；原始使用数据保留在 Apple 本机框架内，不会发送给我们。你可随时在 iOS 设置中撤销授权。

日记建议（可选）

在支持的设备上（iOS 17.2+），你可从「今日」页打开 Apple 的日记建议选择器。仅在你主动选择某条建议时才会保存内容。MobileQuest 不会自动导入你的日记或照片。

通知（可选）

若在设置中开启提醒或里程碑通知，通知完全在本机排程。我们不使用远程推送，通知内容不会经由我们离开你的设备。

iCloud 同步（可选，默认关闭）

开启iCloud 同步后，记录通过你的私有 iCloud（CloudKit 私有数据库）在多设备间同步，绑定你的 Apple ID。我们无法访问这些数据。你可随时关闭。iCloud 的处理受 Apple 隐私政策约束。

我们不做什么

  • 不使用第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK
  • 不在我们控制的服务器上收集你的内容
  • 不出售或共享个人数据
  • 使用应用无需账号、注册或邮箱

数据删除

数据保存在本机（及可选的私有 iCloud）上，删除应用可移除本机数据。要清理 iCloud 中的数据，请在应用内关闭 iCloud 同步，并在 iOS 设置的 iCloud 存储管理中处理。撤销健康或屏幕时间权限可立即停止新的读取。

儿童隐私

MobileQuest 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。我们不会有意收集儿童个人信息，也不会将用户内容传出设备给我们。

政策变更

当应用数据处理方式变化时，我们可能更新本页。文首「最后更新」日期会随之修订。继续使用即表示你接受更新后的政策。
