MobileQuest Support

Last updated: 2026-07-28

MobileQuest is a private, local-first achievement app that turns notes, moods, movement, sleep, and digital balance into tiered collectible badges.

What is new in version 1.1?

Version 1.1 adds Today’s Rhythm, 64 badge series with 168 tiers, richer Health and Screen Time insights, progressive digital-balance achievements, a daily screen budget, and upgraded small and medium Home Screen widgets.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email:

contact@resinbody.com

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue. We usually respond within a few business days.

Common questions

  • Is an account required? No. MobileQuest works without sign-up or login.
  • Does MobileQuest upload my journal or moods? No. Your entries stay on your device by default.
  • Is Health access required? No. Health access is optional and read-only.
  • Is Screen Time access required? No. Screen Time access is optional and processed on device.
  • Why is my Screen Time report empty after authorization? Apple’s private report extension may need a short time to return data after a fresh authorization. Keep Screen Time enabled and reopen the report later.
  • Does the daily screen budget block apps? No. Version 1.1 tracks privacy-safe daily outcomes for achievements and can send an optional local reminder near 75%, but it never blocks apps.
  • Can I turn off permissions? Yes. Health, Screen Time, notifications, and iCloud sync can be managed from Settings.

常见问题

  • 需要账号吗？ 不需要。MobileQuest 无需注册或登录即可使用。
  • 会上传我的日记或心情吗？ 不会。默认情况下，你的记录保存在设备本地。
  • 必须开启健康权限吗？ 不必须。健康权限是可选且只读的。
  • 必须开启屏幕使用时间权限吗？ 不必须。屏幕使用时间是可选功能，并在本机处理。
  • 授权后为什么屏幕时间报告仍然为空？ Apple 的隐私报告扩展在首次授权后可能需要一点时间返回数据。请保持屏幕时间连接，稍后重新打开报告。
  • 每日屏幕预算会阻止 App 吗？ 不会。1.1 只保存用于成就的隐私安全每日结果，并可选择在接近 75% 时发送本机提醒，不会封锁 App。
  • 可以关闭权限吗？ 可以。健康、屏幕使用时间、通知和 iCloud 同步都可在设置中管理。
