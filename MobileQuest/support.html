MobileQuest Support

Last updated: 2026-06-28

MobileQuest is a private, local-first achievement app that turns moods, notes, walks, and quiet wins into collectible badges.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email:

contact@resinbody.com

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue. We usually respond within a few business days.

Common questions

  • Is an account required? No. MobileQuest works without sign-up or login.
  • Does MobileQuest upload my journal or moods? No. Your entries stay on your device by default.
  • Is Health access required? No. Health access is optional and read-only.
  • Is Screen Time access required? No. Screen Time access is optional and processed on device.
  • Can I turn off permissions? Yes. Health, Screen Time, notifications, and iCloud sync can be managed from Settings.

常见问题

  • 需要账号吗？ 不需要。MobileQuest 无需注册或登录即可使用。
  • 会上传我的日记或心情吗？ 不会。默认情况下，你的记录保存在设备本地。
  • 必须开启健康权限吗？ 不必须。健康权限是可选且只读的。
  • 必须开启屏幕使用时间权限吗？ 不必须。屏幕使用时间是可选功能，并在本机处理。
  • 可以关闭权限吗？ 可以。健康、屏幕使用时间、通知和 iCloud 同步都可在设置中管理。
